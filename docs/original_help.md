## Source offset 0x02e0

The Master of Mischief is
planning to take over the
Shady Glen TV (SGTV) station
at midnight.  He has two
helpers.  Telly the robot

patrols the halls.  LiveWire, a magical
piece of electrical cord, guards the
rooms.

The Master of Mischief's hideout is
in one of the rooms.  Can you find
it in time to save the TV station?

## Source offset 0x0aa1

To help you collect clues and capture
secret code, you have an on-screen
calculator, a magic zapper, and your
math and thinking skills.  You get
points for solving math challenges,
answering drill questions, and finding
the Master of Mischief's hideout.

If you want to do only drill questions,
choose Customized Drill for Skill
after you sign in, or press [F2] and
select Play Another Game from the
File menu.

## Source offset 0x0d64

When you press [Space],
energy comes out of your
zapper.  If Telly is lit up
when hit by this energy,
you will be asked drill

questions.

Think and type in your answers quickly,
because the clock ticks on.  If you
give enough right answers, you will
capture one of the 4 pieces of secret
code you need to win the game.

## Source offset 0x12ae

You can see the pieces of secret

## Source offset 0x1381

LiveWire and Telly (when
its belly is not lit up)
disappear when you zap
them with energy.

You can recharge your zapper at
the energy supply machine, but only
after you have collected a new clue.
The energy meter shows how much
energy you have left.  If you win
the game, you get bonus points if
you have energy left over.

## Source offset 0x18bd

The Master of Mischief has put
math challenges--word problems--in
the rooms.  Use [Up] to move up to
large objects in a room to see if
they contain math challenges.

You collect clues by solving math
challenges.  To decide which room
the Master of Mischief is hiding in,
match those clues with the secret
code you capture from Telly the
robot.

## Source offset 0x1a19

solve the math challenges.  You
can work with or without the on-
screen calculator.

You get more points for solving
a challenge correctly on your first
try, but you will be given a hint
after your second try.

## Source offset 0x2409

In the TV station, press [Tab] to use
the decoder.  The room clues and
secret code you have captured are
kept in your decoder.  Use the
Match Rule button to find out the
pattern or sequence that has to be
the same in the set of room clues
and the secret code.

Select the Decide button when you
are sure you know which room the
Master of Mischief's hideout is in.

## Source offset 0x2578

New Super Solvers start out as
Trainees and move up in rank--all
the way to CHAMPION.  Your game
score depends on how well you solve
math challenges and answer drill
questions.  It also depends on what
your rank is and how good you are
at playing and winning the game.

You can see your score in the
lower-right corner of the screen.

## Source offset 0x26cd

Your game score is added to your
Lifetime Score ONLY when you win
a game.  You get points for correctly
solving math challenges and answering
drill questions, for capturing secret
code from Telly, and for correctly
deciding which room the Master of
Mischief's hideout is in.

You get extra points for solving
challenges on the first try and for
having energy or time left over at
the end of a game.

## Source offset 0x2976

Playing Drill for Skill helps you
practice your math facts.  You will
be asked the types of math problems
you selected before you started
to play.  You can return there to
make changes by pressing [F2] and
selecting Play Another Game.
